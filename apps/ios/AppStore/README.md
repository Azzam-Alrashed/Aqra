## English

**Name** [30]: Aqra: Quran Hifz & Revision

**Subtitle** [30]: Revise your hifz, ayah by ayah

**Promotional text** [170]:
The Madinah Mushaf as printed, with tajweed colors. A daily wird measured for you. Stations you climb, one ayah at a time.

**Keywords** [100]: quran,hifz,memorize,revision,mushaf,tajweed,hafiz,tasmee,wird,muraja,madinah,koran,islam

**Description** [4000]:

```
Aqra keeps the Quran memorizer company in memorizing and revising, every day, guided by the hadith:
"It shall be said - meaning to the one who memorized the Qur'an - 'Recite, and rise up, recite (melodiously) as you would recite in the world. For indeed your rank shall be at the last Ayah you recited.'" (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2914, Darussalam translation)

THE MADINAH MUSHAF, AS PRINTED
• All 604 pages in the layout of the 1441H print by the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, so the page you memorized stays in your mind's eye.
• Tajweed colors, which you can turn off.
• What you've memorized takes the color of its topic on the page, growing brighter as it settles.

A DAILY WIRD, MEASURED FOR YOU
• Choose how many pages a day, and Aqra arranges the wird: pages you stumbled on come back first, then a rotation through everything you've memorized, in Mushaf order.
• The memorized ayat are veiled: recite from memory, reveal one ayah at a time, and tap where you stumbled.
• A page comes back after a day, then three, then seven, until it holds.
• Revised in prayer, or to a friend? Check the page off from the wird, and it counts.
• Missed days don't pile up.

CLIMB YOUR STATIONS
• Every ayah you memorize is a station you rise to, in remembrance of the hadith.
• The home shows ten steps, three juz' each, with your share of the Quran, its strength, and your revision streak.
• Progress shows every juz' at a glance: how much you've memorized, and how firmly.

A PLAN THAT FITS YOU
• Tell Aqra where you are and how much you memorize a day, and it sets today's new portion and your expected completion date.

TASMEE'
• Book a seat in a tasmee' session with a teacher who holds an ijazah, in person or by video. Your teacher records where you stumbled.
• Recite to a friend with a code you share, and it counts.
• Race your friends, and share a khatmah by juz'.

NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
• Aqra works without signing in, and your progress stays on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google whenever you like to keep it in your account.

TRUSTED SOURCES
• The Mushaf's text, script and page layout come from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex, unmodified.
```

## Screenshots

`screenshots/` holds five per device and language, in upload order: the welcome hadith, the Mushaf, today's wird
(veiled, one stumble marked), the home, and progress.

| Folder | Device | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `iphone63-ar`, `iphone63-en` | iPhone 6.3" ("iPhone with Dynamic Island, medium display": the slot App Store Connect asks for) | 1206 × 2622 |
| `iphone-ar`, `iphone-en` | iPhone 6.9" originals (iPhone 17 Pro Max); `iphone63-*` are these resized with `sips -z 2622 1206` | 1320 × 2868 |
| `ipad-ar`, `ipad-en` | iPad 13" (iPad Pro 13-inch, M5) | 2064 × 2752 |

They come from the simulators "Aqra Store iPhone" and "Aqra Store iPad", seeded with the sample student from the
landing page's shots (12 juz', a 15-day streak, 8 pages a day): copy `memorization.json`, `revision.json` and
`plan.json` into the app container's `Library/Application Support`, then launch with `-hasSeenOnboarding YES
-memorization.hasDeclared YES -mushaf.lastPage 591 -AppleLanguages "(ar)" -AppleLocale ar_SA` (or `(en)`/`en_US`).
The status bar is set with `simctl status_bar override --time 9:41`. Saved as JPEG, since App Store Connect
rejects screenshots with an alpha channel.

App Store Connect rejects the ﷺ ligature in descriptions ("invalid characters") and support or marketing URLs with a
query string (`?lang=en`), so both listings use the plain URLs.

## App privacy

No tracking. No data used for advertising or analytics. Everything below is linked to the user and used only for
app functionality:

| Data type | Why |
|---|---|
| Contact info: Name | Sign-in name, the display name shown to teachers and friends, a teacher's application |
| Contact info: Email address | Sign in with Apple or Google |
| Contact info: Phone number | A teacher's application ("how to reach you"), optional |
| User content: Photos or videos | Copies of a teacher's ijazah |
| User content: Other user content | Memorization progress and revision backup, tasmee' records, competitions, a teacher's notes |
| Identifiers: User ID | The Firebase account |
| Purchases: Purchase history | Credit packs bought |

Video and audio in video tasmee' are streamed live through LiveKit and never stored, so they aren't "collected".
